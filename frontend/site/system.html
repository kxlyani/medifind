Stock updates waiting in the queue

Pharmacies' updates wait in RabbitMQ until a consumer saves them. Scale the consumers to 0 and this number grows; bring them back and it drains.

Search pods

Each copy of the Search Service checks in every 2 seconds. An even spread of searches is the load balancer at work; new rows appear when the autoscaler adds pods.

    Coordinator nodes

    Three nodes elect a leader with the Bully algorithm. The leader sends a beacon every 2 seconds and hands out the locks that stop two people reserving the same last strip.

    Activity

    Every stock update the consumers save and every reservation, newest first, from all pods.
